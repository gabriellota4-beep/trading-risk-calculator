TRADCAL - Trading Risk Calculator

TRADCAL helps traders calculate risk amounts and position sizes before entering a trade. Proper risk management helps traders protect their capital and maintain consistent trading discipline.

Calculator

How It Works

Enter your account size, the percentage of capital you are willing to risk, and your stop-loss distance. TRADCAL will calculate your risk amount and recommended position size.

Why Risk Management Matters

Risk management is one of the most important principles in trading. Consistent position sizing helps traders avoid excessive losses and preserve capital over the long term.
